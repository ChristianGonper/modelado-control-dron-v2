01 / Project

From the physical model to experimental comparison

The simulator integrates 6DOF rigid-body dynamics, actuators, disturbances, and reproducible trajectories. Two strategies are evaluated on this common basis: classical PID control and hybrid neural control.

The network learns to predict the desired outer-loop force from PID expert demonstrations, while the classical inner loop retains attitude stabilization.

  • 6DOFDynamics and actuators
  • PIDCascaded control
  • MLImitation with MLP, GRU, and LSTM

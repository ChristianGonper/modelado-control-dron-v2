01 / Project

A project in modelling, control, and evaluation

The project frames a control question and builds the environment needed to answer it: a 6DOF rigid-body model with actuators, disturbances, reproducible trajectories, and auditable telemetry.

The methodology tunes PD experts, generates demonstrations, trains MLP, GRU, and LSTM models, and compares their decisions in closed loop. The network replaces the outer loop while the classical inner loop retains attitude stabilization.

  • MODELDynamics, actuators, and disturbances
  • METHODPD experts, imitation, and three architectures
  • EVIDENCEID/OOD comparison, error, and safety
